# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

ZakatBridge exists to serve people with compassion and dignity. We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We welcome people of all faiths and backgrounds who share the goal of transparent, respectful humanitarian work.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming — especially to newcomers
- Respecting differing viewpoints, including differences in religious interpretation
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people the project aims to serve
- Speaking honestly and never exaggerating the project's impact or status

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, including about religion, nationality, or ethnicity
- Sharing anyone's private information without explicit permission
- Adding real beneficiaries' personal data, images, or stories
- Using the project to solicit real money or promote unverified fundraising
- Sectarian or political attacks
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the project maintainers privately (via the contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile, or through GitHub's private reporting features). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining the issue.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for repeated or severe violations.
