Two Ways to Give

Zakat and Sadaqah are kept strictly separate so that every contribution reaches a purpose that matches its intention.

Zakat

Zakat — An Obligation

An obligatory annual contribution (usually 2.5% of qualifying wealth held for one lunar year above the nisab). Zakat may only go to eligible recipients described in the Qur'an (9:60), such as the poor and the needy.

  • Separate tracking from all other funds
  • Only for verified eligible recipients
  • Built-in calculator to estimate what is due
Sadaqah

Sadaqah — Voluntary Charity

Voluntary giving of any amount, at any time, for a broad range of good causes — including education, clean water, and community projects that may not qualify for Zakat.

  • Any amount, any time
  • Wider range of community projects
  • Ongoing charity (Sadaqah Jariyah) projects

How transparency would work

  • Zakat and Sadaqah tracked in separate ledgers

  • Public reports of funds received and delivered
  • Administrative costs disclosed openly
  • Open-source code that anyone can audit

About Us & Our Mission

ZakatBridge began as an idea: many Egyptians living in Canada and the United States want to support families back home, but finding trustworthy, transparent channels is difficult.

This project is an open-source prototype exploring what such a channel could look like. It is not a registered charity, does not hold funds, and has no partner organizations at this stage.

Any future operational version would need to work through properly registered nonprofits in Canada, the United States, and Egypt, and comply with all applicable laws.

Transparency

Open code, open processes, and clear reporting.

Accountability

Every contribution traceable from giver to recipient.

Accessibility

Bilingual, mobile-friendly, and usable by everyone.

Dignity

Beneficiaries are never exposed, shamed, or exploited.
